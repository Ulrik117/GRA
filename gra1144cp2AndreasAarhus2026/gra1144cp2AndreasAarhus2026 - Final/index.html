Toad In The Hole

Toad in the hole is a simple, classic breakfast where a hole is cut in a slice of bread, buttered, and then pan-fried with an egg cracked into the opening. It's a fun way to enjoy toast and eggs with a perfect runny yolk.

Ingredients

  • Two pieces of white Bread
  • Two large Eggs
  • Pad of Butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: Nutella

Instructions

  1. In a small skillet, melt the butter.
  2. Cut a 3-inch hole in the bread and place it in the skillet.
  3. Crack the eggs into the holes. Cook for 2 minutes until browned.
  4. Turn and cook the other side until set. Season to taste.
  5. Optional: Use a spoonful of Nutella between the two circle "scraps" for a sweet treat!
